Import any bank statement. With the AI you already use.

No bank passwords. No aggregator subscriptions. No servers reading your transactions. Gilt turns statement import into a two-minute job for the AI assistant you already have.

How it works

1 · Export the prompt

Gilt prepares a ready-made prompt describing exactly the file format it can verify and import.

2 · Hand it to your AI

Give the prompt plus your bank's PDF or CSV statement to Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant you already use — in their app, under your account, under their privacy terms you already accepted.

3 · Review and import

Gilt validates the file your AI produced — dates, amounts, currencies, duplicates — and shows you every transaction for review before anything is saved.

Why this beats bank connections

  • Works with any bank in the world — if your bank can export a statement, Gilt can import it. Small local banks included.
  • No credentials shared — bank-linking apps ask for your online-banking login via third-party aggregators. Gilt never asks, because it never connects.
  • No middleman servers — there is no Gilt backend to breach. The app itself makes zero network calls to any AI service.
  • You stay in control — every imported transaction is reviewed by you before it lands in your ledger.

Also works in reverse

Want your AI to analyze your spending? Export a structured, privacy-filtered snapshot — merchant names and notes stay out unless you say otherwise — and ask any assistant anything: "Where can I realistically save €100 a month?" Gilt gives you the data; your AI gives you the answers.
