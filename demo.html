A real MCP demonstration

Ask about your money.

In Claude Desktop, we asked Gilt how much was spent on groceries in November 2026, with the transactions that made up the total.

Claude used Gilt’s local MCP connection to read the permitted data. Its answer matched the grocery category in Gilt’s budget: €267.65 out of €400, with €132.35 remaining.

An existing Claude extension connected to the current Gilt 1.8 host. Reporting timezone: Asia/Yerevan. All four transactions were in EUR; no currency conversion was needed.
