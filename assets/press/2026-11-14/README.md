# Gilt — shared budgeting on iPhone and Mac

Captured 14 November 2026 using Gilt 1.8 and synthetic demonstration data.
Website: https://gilt.money/demo.html
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6790835958
Contact: support@gilt.money

Gilt helps couples keep a household budget through their own iCloud accounts. Gilt Family is required to start household sharing. Sharing a ledger and sharing a subscription through Apple Family Sharing are separate steps.

The Mac demonstration used an existing Claude Desktop connection to Gilt's local MCP interface. Claude read four grocery transactions: EUR 60.00 on Nov 3, EUR 86.40 on Nov 4, EUR 60.00 on Nov 10, and EUR 61.25 on Nov 13. Total: EUR 267.65, matching the app. The reporting month was November 2026 in Asia/Yerevan; all amounts were already in EUR.

MCP access is optional and read-only. Users choose permitted accounts and optional transaction details. Setup and a compatible desktop assistant are required. Information permitted by the user is sent to the assistant's AI provider.

Contents: six composed marketing screenshots in app-store/, six original app/client captures in captures/, and one Mac demonstration image in featuring-assets/. Compositions crop and scale real captures and add headings. These are still images, not a video or a live iCloud synchronization test. iPhone and Mac use independently seeded demonstration ledgers. The Mac demonstration used an existing Claude extension with the current Gilt 1.8 host.

These images may be used in editorial coverage of Gilt. Demo balances and transactions are fictional. Gilt is independently developed by Harut Aristakesyan in Yerevan, Armenia, and supports 17 languages.
